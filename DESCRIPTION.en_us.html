Edge Case is a free pixel typeface built from rounded cells. Corners are softened, and where two cells touch only diagonally the join is filled with a concave curve, so every letter reads as one continuous shape rather than a stack of squares.

It is designed on a strict grid of whole and half cells, and is crisp at 16-pixel multiples. The design is capitals-only: lowercase letters map to the same forms. Latin coverage includes accented capitals composed from a consistent set of marks, plus German ẞ, Nordic and Central European letters, punctuation and symbols.

Edge Case was designed by Tejas at 3 Sided Coin. To contribute, see the project repository at github.com/3sidedcoin/edge-case-typeface.

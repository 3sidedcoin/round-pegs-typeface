About Edge Case

Edge Case began as a pixel face called Shade Pixel and was extended and renamed for open release. Each glyph is drawn as an SVG on a four-unit grid, where one unit is a quarter of a pixel cell, and compiled into a font by a small build script kept in the repository.

Drawing rules

Convex corners have a radius of one unit. Where two cells meet only at a corner, a four-pointed concave star joins them. Accent marks are one shape each, centred on the letter, so they land on half-cell positions on purpose.

Using it

Set it at multiples of 16 pixels for crisp rendering. At 8-pixel multiples whole-cell shapes stay crisp and half-cell marks fall between pixels.

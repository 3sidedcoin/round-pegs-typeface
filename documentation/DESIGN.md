# Design rules

- One cell = 4 SVG units = 128 font units (UPM 1024). Half cell = 2 units.
- Cap height is 5 cells (640 units). Letter frames are 20 units high; baseline at the bottom.
- Taller frames: 28 high = baseline at y=24; 32 high = baseline y=28; 36, 40, 44 high = baseline y=32.
- Convex corners: radius 1 unit. Concave fillets and four-pointed stars where cells join only diagonally (radius = half cell).
- Accent marks are one canonical shape each, anchored to the base letter's frame centre (ogonek: right edge), and always land on half-cell offsets.
- Lowercase and accented lowercase map to the same glyphs as capitals.

## Widths

Ink width in cells, advance = ink + 1 cell of space on the right (left side bearing 0):

- 3 cells: I, T (and narrow punctuation).
- 4 cells: stem-and-arm letters (A B C D E F G H J K L P R S), the digits, and most others.
- 5 cells: letters with a diagonal or a centre column: M N V W X Y Z, and the round O, Q, U.

This is a deliberate two-class system, not an accident: the 5-cell O, Q and U keep a 3-cell flat top, which is what makes the face read as round. Narrowing them to 4 cells was previewed and rejected (it makes the rhythm slightly more even but turns the bowls into octagons, and Q needs a redraw).

## Spacing

- Every glyph has a 1-cell gap on its right. Word space is 3 cells (4 cells of white between words).
- Spacing is the grid itself: every letter has ink from 0 to advance minus 1 cell, so the gap between any two letters is 1 cell. That holds for L, F, C, Z and their neighbours too (LC, LE, LJ, LL, LO, LQ, LU, LW, EY, CV, CY all keep the 1-cell gap, so rounded joins and fillet stars never merge). The one exception is three kerning pairs, LV, LY and LT, which move in by 1 cell so the arm of V or Y, or the bar of T, sits flush against the end of L's foot (gap 0). Generated by `sources/tools/kern_rules.py` into `sources/tools/kern.fea`; see `documentation/kerning-pairs.txt`. An earlier automatic 43-pair experiment tightened too much and is kept off in `sources/tools/kern.experimental.fea`.
